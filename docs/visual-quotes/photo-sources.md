## Resolution

The only large originals online are `IMG_8143.jpg` (3024×4032), `IMG_9306.jpg` (2305px) and
`cocktail_bar_home2.jpeg` (2200px). Everything else is 800–1024px. That's fine for cards and the
quote builder, but too small for sharp full-screen scenes or tight logo crops. Enlarging adds no
real detail. The original camera files are needed for those.

The live library also holds seven short real video clips (`2024/05/*.mp4`, `*.mov`). They were not
downloaded or used. They're for later, after the stills are agreed.
